Privacy Policy

HomeSync AI stores your profile details — name, email, mobile number, date of birth, lifestyle answers, and bio — in a Supabase database, used solely to compute and display compatibility matches with other signed-in users.

Your email and mobile number are visible to other users so they can contact you after a match. Your password is never visible to us — Supabase handles authentication and stores only a salted hash.

We do not sell your data to third parties. Since this is a prototype, don't share anything you wouldn't want visible to other users of the app.

Terms & Conditions

Disclaimer

HomeSync AI does not verify the identity of any user. Compatibility scores are a guide, not a guarantee — always meet a potential roommate in person, in a public place, before making any commitment.

This platform is not affiliated with any property listing service, landlord, or housing authority.

Age requirement

HomeSync AI is intended for users aged 18 and above who are seeking shared housing, typically students and young professionals.
