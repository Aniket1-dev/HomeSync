Why we built HomeSync AI

Most roommate hunting still happens through group chats, listing sites built for finding a property rather than a person, and a lot of guesswork. HomeSync AI started as a B.Tech Project-II exploration into whether structured compatibility data — combined with a language model reading between the lines of a bio — could produce better matches than swiping through listings ever could.

Instead of just matching on budget and city, HomeSync AI scores compatibility across sleep schedule, cleanliness, guest frequency, personality, and conflict style — the things that actually make or break living together — then layers an LLM re-ranking pass on top to catch nuance a rigid form can't.

This is a live prototype, not a finished commercial product. We're building it in the open as part of our coursework, and every piece of feedback from real users shapes the next iteration.

How the matching actually works

Each profile is scored against every other candidate in the same city using a weighted similarity formula — closer sleep schedules, cleanliness habits, and personalities score higher, with budget and location as hard filters. The top shortlist can then optionally be re-ranked by an LLM reading both bios together, blending the two into one final score you see on your dashboard.
