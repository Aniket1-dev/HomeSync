How to use HomeSync AI

A practical walkthrough from account creation to finding a compatible roommate, verifying identity, checking a room, coordinating a visit and deciding whether to merge.

The safest path
1. Profile→2. Lifestyle→3. Match→4. KYC→5. Room check→6. Visit→7. Merge
01

Create your account

  1. Sign up with your email or supported sign-in method.
  2. Complete email verification if requested. The signup verification code is 6 digits.
  3. Complete onboarding before expecting meaningful matches.

Use information that describes how you actually live. Matching becomes less useful when answers are optimized to look attractive.

02

Build your roommate profile

Add your name, photo if desired, age/gender where applicable, city, preferred area, budget, move-in timing and a useful bio. Add your location carefully and avoid exposing a sensitive exact home address publicly.

Keep your profile updated when your city, budget or move date changes.

Open Profile
03

Answer the 15 lifestyle questions

The lifestyle questionnaire is the heart of compatibility. Answer the MCQ questions about sleep schedule, cleanliness, guests, personality, smoking/drinking, cooking and conflict style. These answers are used to explain why someone is a strong or weak fit.

Tip: If your preferences genuinely change, update them. Do not change answers just to match a friend.

Update match profile
04

Understand your matches

Your Dashboard ranks potential roommates by compatibility and practical fit. The strongest result is highlighted as your top vibe match where available.

  • Use Best match for compatibility-first ranking.
  • Use Lowest budget when cost is your priority.
  • Use city scope to stay local or, if eligible, expand discovery.
  • Open View profile before contacting someone.
05

Complete KYC / identity verification

Open Workspace → My Profile & Account → Identity & KYC. Upload one supported government identity document and submit it.

Important: Uploaded does not mean verified. An authorized HomeSync administrator manually reviews the document. Only an approved submission should receive the verified status.

Never upload passwords, OTPs, bank credentials or unrelated financial documents.

06

Inspect a room before paying

For room listings, review the rent, deposit, maintenance, electricity rate, room type, availability and available safety/verification information.

  • Ask for original ownership/authorization and rent documents.
  • Confirm the electricity meter and how the tariff is calculated.
  • Check water, washroom, locks, ventilation, network and room condition in person.
  • Ask what is included in rent and what is paid separately.
  • Do not treat an estimated area cost as a guaranteed bill.
Room listings
07

Use maps and navigation safely

When a listing or agreed meeting point has usable coordinates, open the map/navigation action to plan your route. Check the destination yourself before travelling.

For an initial meeting, choose a public location when possible. Live location should only be shared with consent and for a clear purpose.

08

Visit the potential roommate / room

  1. Arrange the visit through the communication flow.
  2. Tell someone you trust where you are going.
  3. Meet in a public place first when possible.
  4. Inspect the actual room rather than relying on photos.
  5. Confirm identity and documents before financial commitments.
09

Raise a merge request

When you think someone is a strong fit, open their profile and use Raise merge request. A merge request is an invitation to explore living together — it is not a lease or payment commitment.

Wait for acceptance. Do not pressure someone who declines or does not respond.

10

Chat after acceptance

After an accepted connection, use the private roommate communication flow to discuss:

  • Budget and how rent/utilities will be split
  • Sleep and quiet hours
  • Cleaning responsibilities
  • Guests and shared spaces
  • Food/cooking arrangements
  • Move-in date and room arrangements
  • Visits and safety expectations

Keep important agreements written down before moving in.

11

Use Premium as a decision toolkit

Premium is not a fake compatibility boost. It adds discovery and decision-support capabilities such as broader search, advanced filters, saved searches/alerts, profile-view insights, match comparison, private shortlists, relocation planning, room-cost workflows and Premium support/concierge where available.

Open Premium Hub
12

Notifications & account controls

Use notifications for new matching activity, profile views, merge requests, chat activity and verification updates where enabled. Use your Profile/Account controls to update information, KYC status and preferences.

You can switch between dark and bright mode from the theme control. Your choice is stored locally for supported pages.

13

If something looks wrong

  • Wrong match → re-check your lifestyle answers and practical filters.
  • Missing profile → refresh and confirm the account/profile is active.
  • KYC stuck → check its status and resubmission reason.
  • Room seems suspicious → stop the transaction and report it.
  • Map is inaccurate → verify the address independently before travelling.
  • Feature appears locked → check whether it requires Premium or an accepted merge.
Contact support →
Trust principle

HomeSync can organize signals and evidence, but it cannot guarantee that a person, property, price, document or meeting is safe. Verify important facts yourself before paying, signing or moving in.
