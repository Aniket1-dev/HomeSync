The problem

Listing sites match you to a property. Nobody matches you to a person.

Most flatmate mismatches don't come from the flat — they come from sleep schedules, cleanliness habits and unspoken expectations that never come up until move-in day. HomeSync AI treats the person-to-person fit as the primary product, not a checkbox filter.

How matching works

A hybrid score: structured, then explained.

Rule-based

Structured fit

Sleep schedule, cleanliness, budget, guest habits and conflict style are weighted and scored numerically — cheap, fast, fully explainable.

LLM-based

Bio understanding

Your free-text bio is read for nuance a form can't capture — like "I work night shifts and need daytime quiet" — and scored with a one-line justification.

Hybrid

One transparent score

Both signals combine into a single compatibility score, with the full breakdown always visible — never a black box.
