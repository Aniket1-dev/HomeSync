# Market-informed roommate analysis

HomeSync should optimize for compatibility, affordability, location/commute, trust, and speed to move. The questionnaire is intentionally limited to 15 high-signal questions.

Primary signals: lifestyle fit, budget overlap, preferred area, commute, move-in timing, recent activity, verification, and dealbreakers.

Secondary roadmap: compare, favorites, safety controls, visit scheduling, roommate agreements, shared expenses, references, room verification, map commute scoring, and personalized re-ranking.
